About Kintsugi Studio 2001

Kintsugi Studio 2001

Fracture a vessel along its inequality metric, then gild the seams with gold.

Pipeline
  1. Choose a bundled preset or upload a watertight .stl / .obj model.
  2. Tune R, the generator, seam width, and seed.
  3. Fracture the mesh, inspect the 3D result, and export ceramic, gold, and 3MF assets.
